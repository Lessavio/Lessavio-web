LESSAVIO / LEGAL

Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

1. Scope

This policy describes the Lessavio presentation website and current desktop preview. Planned web and mobile applications are not yet covered by a released service. Lessavio is an independently maintained project. For questions about how information is handled or to exercise privacy rights, contact valentinholovin@gmail.com.

2. Website hosting and optional analytics

This website is hosted on Vercel. Requests may include your IP address, browser information and technical request logs needed to deliver and secure the website. See Vercel’s Privacy Notice for its processing practices. Download and GitHub links lead to external services with their own policies.

When configured, the website offers optional Google Analytics for Firebase. It loads only after you select “Allow analytics”; declining leaves the website fully usable. Website analytics use a dedicated web configuration, independently of the desktop analytics preference. Collection can include page visits, desktop download clicks, browser/device information, approximate location derived from network information and analytics identifiers. We disable advertising consent, Google signals and ad personalization. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, student records, account identifiers, or page query strings and fragments in our page events.

Your choice is stored in this browser’s local storage. When you allow analytics, Google may set first-party analytics cookies; the site configures their lifetime to one year. You can withdraw consent at any time using “Analytics settings” in the footer. This stops future collection and removes accessible first-party Google Analytics cookies on this host; it does not erase data already processed by Google. Analytics storage and consent choices are separate from desktop settings. Google Analytics may also collect automatic events according to the configured data stream.

We use consent as the basis for optional website analytics. Necessary hosting and security processing supports delivery and protection of the website. Google and Vercel may process information outside your country using safeguards described in their policies. Analytics retention is controlled by the Google Analytics property settings; contact us for information or deletion requests rather than posting private information publicly.

3. Teaching records stay on your device

Student names, subjects, contact information, lesson notes, schedules, prices and payment records are stored in a local desktop database. There is currently no cloud teaching database or synchronization. Local database and backup files are not encrypted by Lessavio. Protect access to your computer and any exported files.

4. Optional accounts

You can use local mode without an account. If you register or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes account credentials and technical information needed for authentication and security. Signing in does not upload your teaching records or encrypt your local files. Account workspaces remain separate on the device. Signing out preserves local records.

5. Optional desktop analytics

Usage analytics are off by default. If you enable them in Settings, Google Analytics for Firebase may process permitted app, screen and feature events, installation identifiers and technical or network metadata. Lessavio’s event policy excludes student records, names, emails, telephone numbers, lesson notes, lesson dates, payment amounts and Firebase user IDs. You can turn analytics off in Settings; doing so stops future collection through this option.

For provider information, see Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and your choices

You control your local records and exports. The app retains automatic workspace backups for 14 days. Deleting a record from the active workspace may not immediately remove it from retained backups, migration snapshots, or copies you exported. Uninstalling the app may leave application data on disk. Account records held by Firebase are separate from teaching records and local files.

7. Requests and contact

Contact valentinholovin@gmail.com for privacy questions, account deletion requests, or requests to access, correct or delete information processed by the project. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to a data protection authority. We may need to verify your request. Do not send passwords or student records. We cannot remotely access or erase your local teaching database; deleting a Firebase account does not erase local files.

8. Your students’ information

Only enter information you are entitled to use for your teaching work. Take particular care with information about children. Lessavio does not need direct accounts for your students in the current desktop version.

9. Updates

We will update this policy when hosting arrangements or product data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
