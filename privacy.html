Privacy Policy

Draft · Updated October 23, 2026

This document is a draft for the current desktop preview. Operator identity, a dedicated privacy contact, and applicable jurisdiction must be confirmed before public launch.

1. Scope

This policy describes the Lessavio presentation website and current desktop preview. Planned web and mobile applications are not yet covered by a released service. This draft is prepared for the Lessavio project; the legal operator and dedicated contact have not yet been specified.

2. This website

The website does not provide registration, collect form submissions, or use analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, or browser storage in its source. Your browser requests the website files from its host. When deployed, that hosting provider may process network information such as IP addresses and request logs. The provider and its retention practices must be identified before publication. Download and GitHub links take you to an external service with its own privacy practices.

3. Teaching records stay on your device

Student names, subjects, contact information, lesson notes, schedules, prices and payment records are stored in a local desktop database. There is currently no cloud teaching database or synchronization. Local database and backup files are not encrypted by Lessavio. Protect access to your computer and any exported files.

4. Optional accounts

You can use local mode without an account. If you register or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes account credentials and technical information needed for authentication and security. Signing in does not upload your teaching records or encrypt your local files. Account workspaces remain separate on the device. Signing out preserves local records.

5. Optional desktop analytics

Usage analytics are off by default. If you enable them in Settings, Google Analytics for Firebase may process permitted app, screen and feature events, installation identifiers and technical or network metadata. Lessavio’s event policy excludes student records, names, emails, telephone numbers, lesson notes, lesson dates, payment amounts and Firebase user IDs. You can turn analytics off in Settings; doing so stops future collection through this option.

For provider information, see Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google’s Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and your choices

You control your local records and exports. The app retains automatic workspace backups for 14 days. Deleting a record from the active workspace may not immediately remove it from retained backups, migration snapshots, or copies you exported. Uninstalling the app may leave application data on disk. Account records held by Firebase are separate from teaching records and local files.

7. Requests and contact

A dedicated route for privacy, account deletion and data requests must be supplied before public launch. General project questions can currently be raised through the Lessavio repository. GitHub is public: do not post passwords, student records or other private information there. An account deletion request does not itself erase local files on your devices.

8. Your students’ information

Only enter information you are entitled to use for your teaching work. Take particular care with information about children. Lessavio does not need direct accounts for your students in the current desktop version.

9. Updates

This document will be revised when the operator details, hosting arrangements or product data practices change. The date above identifies this version.
